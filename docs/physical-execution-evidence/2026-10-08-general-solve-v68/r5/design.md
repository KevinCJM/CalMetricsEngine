# R5: local LU backward envelope without reconstructing LU

## Problem, scope and source facts

R4 has completed all84 fresh-process whole-request measurements. Its extra packing change is insufficient: the4112 case remains slower than the strongest warmed CPU1 NJIT. The terminal private concat solve still reconstructs LU with sampled QR, dense products and a complete source-copy pass merely to initialize an error envelope. A second source pass verifies the final original equation. This design removes the first pass and reconstruction, while retaining the solved equation check and the complete canonical fallback. It changes no operator, public interface, graph dependencies, float64 factor/solution, error policy or eligibility/ownership boundary.

## Numerical model and proof obligation

Primary sources read: [Demmel/Higham LAWN22](https://www.netlib.org/lapack/lawnspdf/lawn22.pdf), conventional componentwise product/triangular-solve models(3),(4) and partitioned GEPP(16); [Higham LAWN104](https://www.netlib.org/lapack/lawnspdf/lawn104.pdf), standard LU solve backward envelope(3.5); [LAPACK DGETRF](https://www.netlib.org/lapack/explore-html/db/d04/group__getrf_gaea332d65e208d833716b405ea2a1ab69.html), right-looking partial-pivot panel, permutations, triangular solve, trailing update. LAWN40 concerns true block LU and is not substituted for scalar GEPP.

The following conservative specialization is our derivation, not a constant quoted from those papers. Let u=2^-53, gamma(k)=ku/(1-ku), and F be the finite computed factors after conventional FP64 GEPP with exact row swaps. Regrouping ordinary multiply/add operations into DGEMM/DTRSM does not change the product leaves: each defining factor equation contains at mostN products. A product path has at most2N rounding factors across products, sums and completed Schur updates; reciprocal-then-multiply panel division adds at most2. Moving the coefficient of the original matrix to the left introduces inverse rounding weights. Bounding both signs gives fewer than4N+4 factors; forN>=1024 gamma(8N) dominates this conservative count. Thus |F_L F_U-P A| <= gamma(8N)|F_L||F_U| plus an absolute underflow allowance. This specialization relies on conventional FP64 BLAS, the existing standard LU algorithm, nearest rounding and the existing finite/range guards. It is not valid for Strassen, reduced precision or arbitrary alternative factorizations. Any future backend must re-establish this model rather than inherit the constant by name.

The positive grouped product G_ig=sum_k |L_ik| max_(j in group_g)|U_kj| bounds every entry in |L||U| in that group. Existing FP64 DGEMM, divided outward by1-gamma(4N), bounds its own rounding. Initialize E_ig=nextafter((gamma(8N)G_ig+1e-270)/(1-gamma(32)),+inf). The1e-270 absolute margin exceedsN^3 times the smallest normal double forN<=8192, covering bounded underflow/flush losses; nonfinite or G>1e100 is rejected. Validate all LAPACK pivot indices even though reconstructing their permutation is no longer needed.

From that initialization retain the entire existing positive-error induction, strict pivot separation, lower diagonal>1e-10 admission versus original1e-14 boundary, finite bounded RHS/substitution checks, and outward margins. It compares the canonical ordered process against F in final permutation coordinates, not an estimated inverse/condition number. Ties, uncertain thresholds, singularity and possible overflow must replay the original scalar-ordered solve. After DGETRS retain full original-coefficient residual checking with its absolute-product rounding bound; rejecting it must restore the coefficient from persistent leaves and replay the original error order. No acceptance from sampled residuals.

## Lifetime and allocation

Keep coefficient in its exclusive private arena, use input-driven C/F construction and retain all input owners. E/full/tail/growth are4*N*group doubles; growth packing and batched recurrence share64N doubles; rhs/diagonal/back substitution need3N. Remove QR and product/representation rectangles and the full permutation vector. Batched updates need two32*group rectangles, replacing three512^2 rectangles. Maxgroups=ceil(N/256)+32. Work capacity remains checked through the existing reserve function and Planner. Native-library512N reserve remains. Persistent source callbacks are retained for the final residual and canonical regeneration. Healthy solve materializes N^2 original bytes once rather than twice. It performs no factor reconstruction copies; concat and independently owned final results keep their existing audit categories.
